What is NAD+, and why does it matter?

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide — NAD+ — is a coenzyme found in every living cell. It powers the mitochondria, fuels DNA repair, and regulates the pathways that keep you feeling energized and sharp.

But NAD+ declines steadily with age. As levels fall, cells lose efficiency — you feel it as fatigue, foggy focus, and slower recovery. Zell‑V NAD+ replenishes this vital reserve directly, at the molecular level where it counts.
